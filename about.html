About Printer Support Center

Printer Support Center is an printer service provider technical-support service focused on helping customers troubleshoot common printer setup, connectivity, software and printing issues.

What we do

Our phone-based support is designed for everyday home and office printer problems. A support representative can listen to the symptoms, understand the device and provide practical troubleshooting guidance.

Our relationship with printer manufacturers

We are an professional support service provider. We are not owned by, operated by, sponsored by, or officially affiliated with Canon, Epson, HP, or other printer manufacturers. Manufacturer names and product images are used only to describe supported products.
